OASIS

2025 / campaign / print

The Golden is a brand based on several pattern iterations I created. I drew inspiration from the curves and edges in art noveau forms. I experimented with different colors and developed one of my patterns into a motion element. I created a brand out of this depicting city lights in the style of art noveau patterns.
