ESSENCE

ESSENCE is a visual identity system created for the Essence Music Festival in New Orleans. The project explores how motion, typography, and light can translate the energy of live music into a contemporary digital campaign.

Inspired by the atmosphere of nightlife and city reflections, the system combines layered type, glowing gradients, and cinematic compositions across print, motion, and social media applications.

Year

2025

Scope

Identity / Motion / Print

Overview

ESSENCE explores how typography and motion can capture the atmosphere of a live music experience. The visual system balances immersive lighting references with bold graphic structure to create a contemporary festival identity.

Across social media assets, animated graphics, and printed materials, the project was designed to feel cinematic, energetic, and cohesive while maintaining a strong visual rhythm.

Tools

After Effects / Photoshop / Illustrator

Role

Creative Direction / Design / Motion
