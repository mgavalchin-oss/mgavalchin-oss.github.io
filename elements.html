ELEMENTS

2025 / campaign / print

I designed the official t-shirt for all students in my dance team’s program, “Elements,” which was worn collectively on stage during performances. Since the team’s identity is heavily rooted in hip-hop culture, I drew inspiration from graffiti aesthetics to create a design that felt expressive, energetic, and authentic to the team’s style. I incorporated bold typography and street-inspired visual elements to match the atmosphere of the performances, while the crown icon served as a recognizable symbol representing the team’s identity and unity.
