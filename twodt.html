Two Designer Timeline

2DT Timeline History of Grahpic Design Advised by Doug Scott Fall 2024 This subway inspired timeline focuses on the design development of Massimo Vignelli and Paul Rand. It is a foldable guide that shows the two timelines simultaniously. Each timeline is read in a different direction allowing the reader to see all of the content on one sheet. Each section is inspired by the works and legacy of the two designers. Vignelli’s portion draws inspiration from the New York subway map assets he designed while Rand’s draws inspiration from his 1950s film posters.

This project took place in Fall 2025.
